Built for a clearer signal

A speed test with nothing to hide.

Three simple measurements

Latency is checked first, then download and upload throughput. Your browser measures each transfer against Cloudflare’s public speed-test endpoint.

Your results stay yours

Test history and theme preferences live in this browser’s local storage. IP and provider details come from ipapi.co and are cached only for this session.

When a test endpoint is unavailable

The app clearly labels a realistic local estimate instead of passing it off as a live result. For a full PWA, browsers require a same-origin service-worker file on HTTPS; a service worker cannot be made installable from a single HTML Blob.
